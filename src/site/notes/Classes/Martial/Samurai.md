# Archetypes
## Bloodhound
#### Bloodhound Stance 
All your attacks deal an extra 4 damage, but you take 4 extra damage from all sources
When you hit a creature with a weapon, the weapon may inflict [Bleed](Conditions) to the target . You can use this extra effect only once per turn.

BUSHIDO: All your attacks become Critical Hits, but you take Double Damage
