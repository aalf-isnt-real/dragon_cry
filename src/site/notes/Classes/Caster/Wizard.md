subclasses: spellcrafter, necromancer, diviner


## Expert's Edge
gain an extra d6 when rolling a chosen skill. This d6 does not count towards the total proficiency in that skill

## METAMAGIC options:
### Fused Hit:
if a spell makes multiple attacks, add all damage die from the attacks together and roll one single attack.

### Expediate Spell
Make a spell cost 1 less AP, to a minimum of 1. This can be used multiple times on the same spell
