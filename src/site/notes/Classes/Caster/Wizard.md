subclasses: spellcrafter, necromancer, diviner

## Magic Pool
You use Spell Points to cast spells
You start with (X) SP, and gain Half your KNO in SP per level up

## Expert's Edge
gain an extra d6 when rolling a chosen skill. This d6 does not count towards the total proficiency in that skill

## Spelltwisting Savant
You are a master of one specific METAMAGIC option, making it better hahahahahhahahaasdajhdwka


## METAMAGIC options:
### Fused Hit:
if a spell makes multiple attacks, add all damage die from the attacks together and roll one single attack.

**SAVANT**: The fused spell deals extra damage as if the unfused spell made one extra attack
### Expediate Spell
Make a spell cost 1 less AP, to a minimum of 1. This can be used multiple times on the same spell

**SAVANT** : Gain 3 free uses
### Switch Style
If the spell's target must roll a Saving Throw, instead make an Attack Roll to determine whether the spell hits or not. If you must make an Attack Roll to determine whether or not the spell hits it's target, instead have the target roll a Reflex Saving Throw.

**SAVANT**: Gain one free use per rest, and may choose any Save instead of changing to a Reflex Save
### Damage Change
Change the damage type of your spell into (Limited Damage Options)

**SAVANT**: Choose 2 damage types from the ones previously given. When changing a spell's damage type into one of the two chosen damage, it does not cost any extra SP. You may also change the damage type to any type, if using extra SP to use Damage Change
