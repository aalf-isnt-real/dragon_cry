Action Points (AP) are the resource used to do almost everything in [[Combat\|Combat]] , such as hitting with your weapon, casting a spell, using a skill or ability, moving, etc.

At the start of combat, you have ALL your AP (if thats too crazy, start with half minimum of 2)
You regain half your AP, round up, on the start of your next turn (or end of current turn (playtesting time!!!!!!!!!))

- 2 ap - 1-3
- 3 ap - 4-9
- 4 ap - 10-14
- 5 ap - 15-19
- 6 ap - 20
