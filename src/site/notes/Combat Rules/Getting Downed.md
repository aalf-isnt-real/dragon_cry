In combat, whenever you reach 0 hit points, you get Downed and receive the [Dying Condition](Conditions)

Dying always includes a value, and if it ever reaches dying 4, you die
If your Dying Value reaches Dying 0, you remain stable and can stop rolling death saves and receive the [Stable Condition](Conditions)

If you are [Stable](Conditions) and at 0 Hit Points, you return to 1 Hit Point and awaken after sufficient time passes. The GM determines how long you remain Stable, from a minimum of 10 minutes to several hours. If you are healed, you lose the [Stable Condition], but gain one stack of [Wounded](Conditions) and can act normally on your next turn.

At the start of every turn while Dying, you must roll a Death saving throw. This is a Flat Check with a DC equal to 10 + current Dying Value + current Wounded value.

On FAIL, your Dying Value increases by 1
On SUCCESS, your Dying Value decreases by 1
On Nat1, Your Dying Value increases by 2
On Nat20, Your Dying Value decreases by 2
