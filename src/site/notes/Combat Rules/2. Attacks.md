In [[Combat\|Combat]], you may use any weapon to make an attack

Multi attack penalty after already having attacked something
- -5 on second attack
- -10 on third attack and above
OR
- remove 1 proficiency die per extra attack (evil)

Roll 3 of the same number OR 10 above enemies ac then CRIT!
