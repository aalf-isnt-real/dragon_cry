There are many conditions that can occur in [[Combat\|Combat]]

Dying: You are prone and start making Death Saving Throws, on Dying 2 you are unconscious instead of prone

Stable

Unconscious

Wounded: Comes with a value that increases with every stack. Decreases all rolls by current value, and increases the DC of Death saves by its value
