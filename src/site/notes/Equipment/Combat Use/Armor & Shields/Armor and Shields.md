Armor Base + Agility (up to armor cap) + Armor Training Mod for type you're wearing
+other bonuses + penalties
## Armor
Increases AR (armor rating)
Armor has health (called protection) that acts like dnd5e temp hp
Armor "breaks" when it reaches 0 protection, reducing AR
Can be fixed during resting/off time (heals protection)
some armour could have extra effects before breaking, or after breaking
## Unarmored
Armor Base: 1
Agility Mod: Full AGI 

1 + AGI + Training
## Light Armors
Armor Mod + AGI + Light Armor Training
## Medium Armor
Armor Mod +1/2 AGI + Med. Armor Training
## Heavy Armor
Armor Mod + Heavy Armor Training
## Shields
Can raise a shield
