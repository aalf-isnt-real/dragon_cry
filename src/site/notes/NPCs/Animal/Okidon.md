(Okyl-Dono)
Scale Armour Lizard

Small armoured lizard found in the Bastarin Region
They can roll into a ball like an armadillo
They have a projectile tongue, that shoots out to catch prey
They have a weird beak thing
Their forelegs are more developed than their hind legs, as they drag themselves forward to move
They stand on their long tail to observe their surroundings and scope out potential prey

Diet consists of insects and smaller lizards
